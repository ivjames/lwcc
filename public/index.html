Opening Announcements

Welcome to Worship — Rev. Johan Dodge

Good Morning and Welcome to Worship! Worship is often the first place that new people come into contact with this growing community of faith. And so, if you are joining us for the first time, I say to you what is said every Sunday: You are welcome here. Here is a place where all are welcome. For such is the love of God for God's Very Good Creation.

Call to Worship: Doxology

Praise God from whom all blessings flow; praise God, all creatures here below; praise God above, ye Heav'nly host: praise Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. Amen.

Prayer of the Day: "The Prayer of Jesus" — Phyllis Spencer

(Translated from Aramaic, the language of first-century Galilee and Judea.)

O cosmic Birther of all radiance and vibration, soften the ground of our being and carve out a space within us where your Presence can abide. Fill us with your creativity so that we may be empowered to bear the fruit of your mission. Let each of our actions bear fruit in accordance with our desire. Endow us with the wisdom to produce and share what each being needs to grow and flourish. Untie the tangled threads of destiny that bind us, as we release others from the entanglement of past mistakes. Do not let us be seduced by that which would divert us from our true purpose, but illuminate the opportunities of the present moment. For you are the ground and the fruitful vision, the birth, power, and fulfillment, as all is gathered and made whole once again. And So It Is!

Hymn: "Be Thou My Vision"

Prayers of Intercession — Rev. Johan Dodge

Compassionate God, you are the source of all life and all love. Help us to listen to your leading and share what we have with a hungry and hurting world.

We pray for the earth and all the creatures who share this planet together. May we do our part in restoring the balance of your creation and deepen our commitment to follow the way of Jesus by feeding and serving others.

Jesus said, "they need not go away; you give them something to eat."

We pray for all the people of the world, for their ethnic groups, nations, and leaders, that wars will cease, that the hungry will be fed, and that refugees will return home in safety and in peace.

Jesus said, "they need not go away; you give them something to eat."

We pray for all who suffer from physical ills and for all those who struggle for personal identity and spiritual peace. May they find their truth without money and their peace without cost.

Jesus said, "they need not go away; you give them something to eat."

Gracious and merciful God, abounding in steadfast love, we join our voices with all that you have created in speaking your praises and blessing your name. Amen.

Matthew 14:13-21

13Now when Jesus heard this, he withdrew from there in a boat to a deserted place by himself. But when the crowds heard it, they followed him on foot from the towns. 14When he went ashore, he saw a great crowd, and he had compassion for them and cured their sick. 15When it was evening, the disciples came to him and said, "This is a deserted place, and the hour is now late; send the crowds away so that they may go into the villages and buy food for themselves." 16Jesus said to them, "They need not go away; you give them something to eat." 17They replied, "We have nothing here but five loaves and two fish." 18And he said, "Bring them here to me." 19Then he ordered the crowds to sit down on the grass. Taking the five loaves and the two fish, he looked up to heaven and blessed and broke the loaves and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the crowds. 20And all ate and were filled, and they took up what was left over of the broken pieces, twelve baskets full. 21And those who ate were about five thousand men, besides women and children.

Message: "Love Unleashed" — Rev. Johan Dodge
Questions for Reflection — Rev. Johan Dodge

Sometimes the miracle is simply an act of kindness, an openness to the other, or an opportunity to share and receive together. How does this reimagining of the miracle, grounded in every day life, free you to think of God acting in the world through our hands and feet?

Reflection: "His Eye Is On the Sparrow" — Hannah Yi, pianist
Invitation to the Offering — Phyllis Spencer

As we abide in God, we are blessed and have been blessed with a wealth unimaginable in so many parts of the world where God's people also abide in God. It is our task then, to recognize what God has shared with us and to share with those in need around us, recognizing that the answer to the question: "Who is my neighbor?" is all of creation everywhere. Amen.

Prayer of Thanksgiving — Phyllis Spencer

Loving and Generous God, you provide for us and even bless us in more ways than we will ever remember. We give these gifts as a sign of our love in you knowing that these humble gifts will be blessed and magnified as we share what we have with one another. May these gifts and our very lives as we share in life together tell of your wondrous and steadfast love. Amen.

Prayer Journal

Use this prayer in your time with God each day.

Household Prayer: Morning

On this new day, O God, make me watchful for where I may be your love in the world; keep me grounded in your love that I may follow in your way in all things; and hold me in your grace, as I am sure to stumble. Amen.

Consider The Upper Room Daily Devotional as a resource for Midday Prayer.
Household Prayer: Evening

Hear me at the end of this day, O God, help me to entrust my cares to you. Grant me grace for my failings and rest for my weary soul, that I may be strengthened to do your will and share your love when a new day dawns. Amen.
